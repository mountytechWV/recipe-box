Getting Started

Recipe Box is a place to keep all your favorite recipes, like a recipe card box on your kitchen counter, but on your computer, tablet, or phone. It's free, it works without internet, and your recipes stay private to you.

Getting set up takes three steps: open Recipe Box, add it to your device, and then add your recipes. Please do them in this order.

Step 1: Open Recipe Box

  1. Open the program you use to visit websites. This is called a web browser.
    • On a computer, use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
    • On an iPad or iPhone, use Safari (the blue compass).
    • On an Android tablet or phone, use Chrome.
  2. Tap or click in the long bar at the very top of the screen where website addresses go.
  3. Type this address exactly: mountytechwv.github.io/recipe-box
  4. Press Enter on a keyboard, or tap Go.

You should see a screen that says Welcome to Recipe Box. Don't choose either of the two options on it yet. Install Recipe Box first (Step 2). The Welcome screen shows the install steps for your device in a blue box, and the steps are below too.

Step 2: Add Recipe Box to your device

This puts a Recipe Box icon on your screen, just like your other apps, so you never have to type the address again. You only do this once.

On an iPad or iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button. It looks like a square with an arrow pointing up. On an iPad, it's at the top of Safari. On an iPhone, it's at the bottom of the screen; if you don't see it, tap the ⋯ button first.
  2. A list will open. Scroll down past Copy and Add to Bookmarks.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner.
  5. Close Safari. You'll see a red Recipe Box card on your Home Screen. Tap it to open Recipe Box. From now on, always open Recipe Box this way, not from Safari.

Don't see "Add to Home Screen"? Scroll to the very bottom of the list, tap Edit Actions, and add it from there.

On a computer, using Chrome or Edge

  1. Look at the right end of the address bar at the top of the screen for a small install icon. In Chrome, it looks like a little computer screen with a down arrow. In Edge, it looks like three small squares and a plus sign.
  2. Click it, then click Install.
  3. If you don't see the icon, click the browser's menu (the three dots ⋮ or … in the top right corner) and look for Install Recipe Box. In Chrome, it may be under Cast, save, and share. In Edge, it may be under Apps.

Recipe Box will open in its own window, and an icon will be added to your computer so you can open it anytime.

On a Mac, using Safari

  1. Click the File menu at the top of your screen.
  2. Choose Add to Dock, then click Add.
  3. Close Safari and open Recipe Box from its new icon in the Dock. From now on, always open it this way.

Some browsers show a red Install button on the Welcome screen instead. If you see it, click Install and follow the prompt. That's all there is to it.

On an Android tablet or phone

  1. Tap the three dots ⋮ at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install or Add to confirm.
  4. Open Recipe Box from its new icon on your home screen.

Forgot this step? No problem. Once you're in Recipe Box, a blue box near the top says Install Recipe Box. Tap Show me how and it will walk you through it.

Step 3: Add your recipes

Open Recipe Box from its new icon. You'll see the Welcome to Recipe Box screen with two choices.

If someone sent you a recipe file

A family member may have emailed you a file of their recipes. It will have a name ending in .json.

  1. First, save the file from your email.
    • On a computer, click the attachment and choose Download. It will go in your Downloads folder.
    • On an iPad or iPhone, tap the attachment, tap the Share button, and choose Save to Files.
    • On Android, tap the attachment and choose Download.
  2. In Recipe Box, tap Restore from a backup file.
  3. Find the file you saved and tap it.

In a few seconds, the recipes will appear in your recipe box.

If you're starting fresh

Tap Start with an empty recipe box. Then tap Add your first recipe to type in a favorite.

Already got past the Welcome screen? You can still load a backup file anytime. Tap the settings button (three small sliders at the top of the screen), then tap Restore from a backup file.

A Quick Tour of Recipe Box

Think of Recipe Box as a recipe card box with dividers. Every recipe is a card, filed in alphabetical order by its name.

The bar across the top

This bar stays at the top of every screen, so you can always get where you need to go. (On a phone, Browse, Index, Meal plan, and Add move to the bottom of the screen.)

  • Browse takes you to your recipe box. This is the main screen.
  • Index shows all your recipes in one list, like the index in the back of a cookbook.
  • Meal plan is a calendar for the week where you can plan what to cook each day.
  • The search box finds recipes by name or by ingredient. Type "chicken," for example, to see every recipe that uses chicken.
  • The green "Saved" label (on wide computer screens only) tells you your changes are saved. Whether or not you see it, Recipe Box saves your recipe box automatically.
  • The cloud button appears only if you turn on sync. It shows how syncing is going.
  • The shopping cart opens your shopping list.
  • The question mark opens this help guide.
  • The settings button looks like three small sliders. This is where you make backups and change how the app looks.
  • The red "Add recipe" button lets you add a new recipe.

The Browse screen

At the top it says "What's cooking today?" Below that are three ways to find a recipe.

Categories (on the left side). Tap All to see every recipe, or Favorites to see only the ones you've marked as favorites. Any categories you've made, like Desserts or Soups, also show up here.

Difficulty. Slide the two red circles to show only recipes in a certain range, from Very easy to Advanced. Leave them at both ends to see everything.

The letter tabs. These work like the dividers in a recipe box. Tap a letter to see every recipe whose name starts with that letter. Recipes that start with a number, like "7 Layer Salad," are under the # tab.

Each recipe appears as a card with its name in a colored band at the top. Tap a card to open it.

Inside a recipe

When you open a recipe, you'll see its total time, servings, and difficulty at the top, then buttons for Start cooking, Add to Favorites, Add to Shopping list, Add to Meal plan, Share, Print, and Edit. The ingredients are on the left and the steps on the right. Below them you'll find The story behind it (if the recipe has one) and Times we've made it. To go back, tap Back to recipes at the top left.

Cooking mode

Tap Start cooking and the recipe shows one step at a time in large print, so it's easy to read from across the kitchen. If a step mentions a time, a timer button appears. Tap Read aloud and Recipe Box reads each step to you.

Adding your own recipes

Tap the red Add recipe button. You can type a recipe in yourself, or bring one in from a website, a cookbook (scan it with your tablet or phone camera), or a shared file someone sent you. Recipe Box fills in the form for you. Either way, you'll get a chance to look it over before tapping Save recipe.

Meal plan and Shopping list

The Meal plan shows the days of the week. Tap Add a recipe under any day to plan a meal. When you add recipes to your Shopping list, the ingredients are gathered into one list you can take to the store, and you can add other items too, like paper towels.

What can I make?

Not sure what to cook? Tap What can I make? on the Browse screen and tell Recipe Box what you have in your kitchen. It shows the recipes that use those ingredients. See What Can I Make?

Don't worry about breaking anything

Everything you do is saved automatically. If you delete a recipe by mistake, you have 30 days to put it back from Recently deleted. If you ever get lost, tap Browse to get back to your recipe box. And if you make a backup now and then (see Backups and Settings), your recipes are always safe.

Want a quick look around? The first time you open Recipe Box, a short tour shows you the main features. You can take it again anytime from settings by tapping take the quick tour.

Finding Your Recipes

There are four ways to find a recipe in Recipe Box. Use whichever feels easiest to you.

1. Flip through the letter tabs

This is just like flipping through the dividers in a recipe card box.

  1. Tap Browse at the top of the screen. (On a phone, Browse is at the bottom of the screen.)
  2. Find the row of letters, # and A through Z.
  3. Tap a letter. You'll see a card for every recipe whose name starts with that letter.
  4. Tap a card to open the recipe.

A few tips:

  • The small number under each letter tells you how many recipes are filed there. A little dot means there are none.
  • Recipes are filed by the very first letter of their name. "The Best Meatloaf" is under T, not M.
  • Recipes that start with a number, like "7 Layer Salad," are under #.
  • On a phone, the letters don't all fit across the screen. Slide your finger left or right on the letters to see the rest.

2. Pick a category

Categories are groups of recipes, like Desserts or Soups. On the Browse screen, you'll see them listed on the left side. (On a phone, they're in a row near the top. Slide the row left to see more.)

  • Tap All to see every recipe.
  • Tap Favorites to see only the recipes you've marked as favorites.
  • Tap any other category, like Desserts, to see just those recipes.

The number next to each one tells you how many recipes it holds.

You choose a recipe's categories when you add or edit it. A recipe can be in more than one, so a pie could be in both Desserts and Holidays.

3. Search for it

The search box is at the top of the screen and says Search recipes or ingredients.

  1. Tap the search box.
  2. Type part of the recipe's name, or an ingredient it uses. For example, type carrot to find every recipe that has carrots in it.
  3. Matching recipes appear right away. Each card shows why it matched, like Has Carrots.
  4. Tap a card to open it.

When you're done, tap Clear search to see all your recipes again. (Some devices also show a small × at the end of the search box that does the same thing.)

4. Use the Index

The Index is a simple list of every recipe, like the index in the back of a cookbook. It's handy when you'd rather read names than look at cards.

  1. Tap Index at the top of the screen.
  2. Choose how you'd like the list arranged:
    • A to Z lists every recipe in alphabetical order. Tap a letter near the top to jump straight to it.
    • By category groups the recipes under each category, such as Desserts or Soups.
  3. Tap any recipe name to open it.

Narrowing by difficulty

On the Browse screen there's a Difficulty slider with two red circles, from Very easy to Advanced.

  • To see only easy recipes, slide the right-hand circle toward Very easy.
  • To see everything again, slide both circles back to the ends. It will say Any difficulty.

Below the slider is a checkbox that says Include recipes not rated yet. Leave this checked. If you uncheck it, any recipe that hasn't been given a difficulty rating will disappear from view, which may be most of them.

Can't find a recipe?

  • Is there a word in the search box? Tap Clear search.
  • Is a category other than All selected? Tap All.
  • Has the difficulty slider been moved, or is Include recipes not rated yet unchecked? Slide the circles back to the ends and check the box.
  • Did you look under the right letter? Remember, "The," "A," and "My" count as the first word.

Viewing and Cooking a Recipe

Opening a recipe

Tap any recipe card on the Browse screen, or any recipe name in the Index. The recipe opens with everything you need on one screen. To go back to all your recipes, tap Back to recipes at the top left.

The top of the recipe

Right under the recipe's name you'll see three boxes.

Total time is about how long the recipe takes from start to finish.

Servings is how many people the recipe feeds. You can change this:

  • Tap + to make more. Tap − to make less.
  • All the ingredient amounts change to match. For example, if you double the servings, 1 cup of sugar becomes 2 cups.
  • A note under Ingredients reminds you that the amounts have been changed, and the servings box shows the original number, like "30 (from 24)."
  • This doesn't change your saved recipe. It's only for this time you're cooking it.

Difficulty shows how hard the recipe is, using little chef hats. If it says Not rated yet, you can rate it yourself: tap the first hat for Very easy, up to the fifth hat for Advanced.

The buttons

  • Start cooking opens cooking mode, explained below.
  • Add to Favorites adds the recipe to your Favorites, so you can find it quickly from the Browse screen. The button then says Favorite. Tap it again to take it back out.
  • Add to Shopping list and Add to Meal plan are explained in Meal Plan and Shopping List.
  • Share is explained in Sharing a Recipe.
  • Print is explained in Printing a Recipe.
  • Edit lets you change the recipe.

If the recipe doesn't have a picture yet, you'll see a box that says Add a photo. Tapping it opens the recipe for editing so you can add one.

Ingredients and steps

The Ingredients are listed on the left, each with a small box next to it. As you gather each ingredient, tap its box to check it off. The Steps are on the right, numbered in the order you do them.

The story behind it

Some recipes come with a story: who made it, when it was served, or a memory that goes with it. If a recipe has one, you'll see it under the steps, written like a note on a recipe card. To add or change a story, tap Edit and fill in The story behind this recipe near the bottom.

Times we've made it

This keeps track of every time you make a recipe, so over the years it becomes a little cooking diary.

  1. After you make a recipe, open it and scroll down to Times we've made it.
  2. Tap We made this.
  3. Today's date is filled in for you. Change it if you made it on another day.
  4. If you like, add a note, like "Doubled the garlic, everyone had seconds."
  5. Tap Add to our log.

The newest times show first. To remove one, tap the × next to it. If you remove one by mistake, tap Undo right away.

When you finish a recipe in cooking mode and tap All done, Recipe Box asks if you'd like to add it to your log. Tap Not now to skip it.

Cooking mode

Cooking mode is made for when you're standing at the counter. It shows one step at a time in large print.

To start: tap the red Start cooking button.

Moving through the steps:

  • Tap Next step to go forward, or Back to go to the previous step.
  • At the top, you'll see which step you're on, like Step 2 of 6.
  • On a tablet or phone, you can also swipe left or right. On a computer, you can use the arrow keys.

Ingredients for each step: under each step, In this step lists the ingredients that step uses, with their amounts.

Your screen stays on: on most devices, the screen won't go dark while you're in cooking mode. You'll see Screen stays on when this is working.

Hearing the steps read aloud: tap Read aloud under the step. Recipe Box reads the step out loud, then reads each new step as you move to it. Tap Read this step again to hear it once more, or tap Read aloud is on to turn it off. Recipe Box remembers your choice next time. Make sure your device's volume is up.

When you're finished: on the last step, tap All done. Recipe Box asks if you'd like to add it to Times we've made it. You can also tap Exit at the top anytime.

Using a timer

When a step mentions a time, like "Bake for 25 minutes," a timer button appears under that step.

  1. Tap the timer button, for example Start 25 minutes timer.
  2. The timer appears near the top of the screen and counts down.
  3. When time is up, it beeps three times and says Time is up.

Good to know:

  • If a step gives a range, like "9 to 11 minutes," the timer is set for the longer time. Check on your food at the shorter time.
  • You can run more than one timer at once.
  • To stop a timer early, tap Cancel next to it.
  • Make sure your device's volume is turned up so you can hear the beep.
  • Keep cooking mode open while a timer is running.

Adding and Editing Recipes

You can type a recipe in yourself, like copying a recipe card by hand. Or, if the recipe is on a website or in an email, you can paste it in, and Recipe Box fills out the form for you. You can also scan a recipe from a cookbook, or add one someone sent you. Either way, you'll get to look everything over before it's saved.

Typing in a recipe

  1. Tap the red Add recipe button at the top of the screen. (On a phone, tap Add at the bottom.)
  2. A form called Add a recipe opens. Fill in as much as you like. Only the name is really needed, and you can always come back and add the rest later.
  3. When you're finished, scroll to the bottom and tap Save recipe.

Here's what each part of the form is for:

  • Recipe name. This is how the recipe is filed. It goes under the first letter of the name, so "Grandma's Pound Cake" is filed under G.
  • Short description. A sentence or two about the dish, if you'd like.
  • Total time (minutes). About how long it takes from start to finish. Type just the number, like 45. For an hour and a half, type 90.
  • Servings. How many people it feeds. This is what lets you make the recipe bigger or smaller later.
  • Makes (optional). For things that aren't counted in servings, like 2 dozen cookies or one 9-inch pie.
  • From. Where the recipe came from, like Grandma, a cookbook, or a website.
  • Web link (optional). If the recipe came from a website, you can put its address here.
  • Difficulty. Tap how hard the recipe is, from Very easy to Advanced. If you tap one by mistake, tap it again to clear it.
  • Category. How you group recipes, like Desserts or Soups. See Adding categories below.
  • Favorite. Tap Mark as a favorite to add the recipe to your Favorites. The button then says Favorite. Tap it again to take it back out.
  • Photo. Tap Choose a photo to add a picture of the dish. Recipe Box makes it smaller automatically so it doesn't take up much room.

Adding the ingredients

Each ingredient has four small boxes:

  • Amount: how much, like 2, 1 1/2, or ¾. You can also type a range like 2 to 3.
  • Unit: the measurement, like cups, tsp, or lb. Leave it empty for things you count, like 2 eggs.
  • Ingredient: what it is, like Flour.
  • Note: anything extra, like chopped or softened.

To add another ingredient, tap Add ingredient.

Recipe Box helps you as you type. When you start typing an ingredient, a list pops up with ones you've used before. Type chick, for example, and you'll see Chicken, Chicken Broth, and so on. Tap the right one. If what you're typing is new, tap Add new ingredient instead.

"Did you mean...?" If you misspell an ingredient, Recipe Box may ask, for example, Did you mean Buttermilk? Tap the suggested word to fix it. If your spelling is what you meant, you don't need to do anything. Just keep going, and Recipe Box will add it as a new ingredient. This keeps your recipe box tidy, so searching for an ingredient finds every recipe that uses it.

Adding the steps

Type the first step in the box labeled Step 1. Tap Add step for the next one, and so on.

Put just one step in each box. It makes cooking mode much easier to follow, since it shows one box at a time.

Got one long paragraph instead of steps? Tap Split into separate steps under it, and Recipe Box will break it into one step per sentence. Look them over afterward to make sure they split in the right places.

Headings, rearranging, and removing

Headings: some recipes have parts, like a cake and its frosting. Tap Add section heading to add a title like Frosting, then put that part's ingredients or steps below it.

Rearranging and removing: next to each ingredient and step are three small buttons. The up arrow moves it up, the down arrow moves it down, and the trash can removes it.

The extras at the bottom

Equipment you'll need is for anything special, like a 9x13 pan or a stand mixer. Tips and notes is for anything else worth remembering, like "Freezes well." The story behind this recipe is for the memories that go with it: who made it, when it was served, or why it's special.

Adding categories

  1. In the Category part of the form, tap + Add category.
  2. A list of all your categories appears in alphabetical order, such as Appetizers, Breakfast, and Dessert. Tap the one you want. If the list is long, start typing the name to narrow it down.
  3. To make your own, type its name, like Holidays, and press Enter (or tap the new name in the list).
  4. To take a category off a recipe, tap the small × next to its name.

Pasting a recipe from a website or email

  1. Go to the recipe on the website, or open the email.
  2. Select the recipe's text, starting at the name and ending after the last step:
    • On a computer, click just before the recipe name, hold the mouse button down, and drag to the end of the last step. Then press Ctrl and C together (on a Mac, Command and C).
    • On a tablet or phone, press and hold on the first word until it's highlighted, drag the little handle down to the end of the last step, and tap Copy.
  3. In Recipe Box, tap Add recipe.
  4. Near the top of the form, under Bring in a recipe from, tap A website. The Paste a recipe screen opens.
  5. Paste into the Recipe text box:
    • On a computer, press Ctrl and V together (on a Mac, Command and V).
    • On a tablet or phone, press and hold inside the box and tap Paste.
  6. If you'd like, paste the website's address in Web link.
  7. Tap Read this recipe.

Recipe Box fills out the form for you. Look it over before saving, since sometimes a line lands in the wrong spot. Fix anything that looks off, then tap Save recipe.

There's an even faster way to save recipes from websites. See Saving Recipes from Websites.

Changing a recipe

  1. Open the recipe and tap Edit.
  2. Make your changes.
  3. Tap Save changes at the bottom.

If you don't want to keep your changes, tap Back to recipe or Cancel instead. Be careful: Recipe Box won't ask "Are you sure?" It leaves without saving anything you changed.

Deleting a recipe

  1. Open the recipe and tap Edit.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete recipe.
  3. Recipe Box asks Delete this recipe? Tap Delete recipe again to confirm.

The recipe moves to Recently deleted, where it waits for 30 days in case you change your mind. Right after deleting, you can also tap Undo at the bottom of the screen.

Getting a deleted recipe back

  1. Open settings and tap Recently deleted.
  2. Find the recipe and tap Put it back.

Each recipe shows how many days are left. After 30 days, it's removed for good. You can also tap Delete forever to remove one right away, or Empty Recently deleted to clear them all.

Good to know: Recently deleted is kept on each device separately. If you use sync and delete a recipe on your computer, it also shows up in Recently deleted on your tablet, so you can put it back from either one.

Saving Recipes from Websites

If you find recipes online, there's an easier way to get them into Recipe Box than copying and pasting by hand. You add a special bookmark to your web browser once, and after that it does most of the work for you.

  • On a computer, the bookmark is called Save to Recipe Box. One click sends the recipe straight to Recipe Box with the form filled in.
  • On a tablet or phone, the bookmark is called Copy Web Recipe. One tap copies the recipe, and then you paste it into Recipe Box.

Find your device below. You only need to set it up once.

On a computer (Chrome or Edge)

Step 1: Show your bookmarks bar. This is the thin strip just under the address bar at the top of your browser.

  • On Windows, press Ctrl, Shift, and B together.
  • On a Mac, press Command, Shift, and B together.

If the bar disappears instead, it was already showing. Press the same keys again to bring it back.

Step 2: Drag the button onto the bar.

  1. In Recipe Box, click the settings button (three small sliders at the top).
  2. Scroll down to Save recipes from websites.
  3. Find the button that says Save to Recipe Box. Click on it and hold the mouse button down.
  4. While still holding, move your mouse up to the bookmarks bar.
  5. Let go.

Clicked the button instead of dragging it? Nothing will happen. Try again, holding the mouse button down while you move.

Using it:

  1. Go to a recipe on any website.
  2. Click Save to Recipe Box on your bookmarks bar.
  3. Recipe Box opens in a new tab with the recipe filled in.
  4. Look it over, fix anything that came through wrong, and click Save recipe.

On a tablet or phone

Step 1: Copy the bookmark's code.

  1. Open Recipe Box from your Home Screen.
  2. Tap the settings button (three small sliders at the top).
  3. Scroll down to Save recipes from websites and tap Set up Copy Web Recipe.
  4. Under Which are you using?, make sure the right choice is selected. Recipe Box picks one for you, but tap a different one if it's wrong.

Step 2: Follow the steps for your device below.

iPad or iPhone, using Safari

Make the bookmark

  1. Open Safari and go to any website. Any page is fine.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up), then tap Add Bookmark. On an iPhone, if you don't see the Share button at the bottom, tap ⋯ first.
  3. Change the name to Copy Web Recipe. Under Location, choose Favorites. Tap Save.
  4. Open your bookmarks. On an iPad, tap the sidebar button at the top left, then Bookmarks. On an iPhone, tap the Bookmarks button (an open book) at the bottom, or tap ⋯ first if you don't see it.
  5. Open Favorites, tap Edit, then tap Copy Web Recipe.
  6. Tap the web address under the name, then tap the small ⓧ to clear it. Press and hold in the empty box and tap Paste.
  7. Tap Done.

On an iPad, show the Favorites bar: open the iPad's Settings app, tap Safari, and turn on Show Favorites Bar.

Use it

  1. Go to a recipe on any website in Safari.
  2. On an iPad, tap Copy Web Recipe on the Favorites bar under the address. On an iPhone, tap the Bookmarks button, then Favorites, then Copy Web Recipe.
  3. Tap Copy this recipe, then paste it into Recipe Box (see Pasting the recipe below).

iPad or iPhone, using Chrome

Make the bookmark

  1. Open Chrome and go to any website. Any page is fine.
  2. Tap the ⋯ menu, then tap Add to Bookmarks.
  3. Tap ⋯ again, then Bookmarks. Open Mobile Bookmarks and find the bookmark you just made.
  4. Press and hold that bookmark, then tap Edit Bookmark.
  5. Change the name to Copy Web Recipe.
  6. Clear the web address, press and hold in the empty box, and tap Paste.
  7. Tap Done.

Use it

  1. Go to a recipe on any website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the address bar at the top and type Copy Web Recipe.
  3. In the list that drops down, tap Copy Web Recipe (it has a star next to it).
  4. Tap Copy this recipe, then paste it into Recipe Box (see Pasting the recipe below).

Chrome has no bookmarks bar on tablets and phones, and tapping this bookmark from the Bookmarks list will not work. Always start it from the address bar.

Android tablet or phone, using Chrome

Make the bookmark

  1. Open Chrome and go to any website. Any page is fine.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then tap the star ☆ at the top of the menu.
  3. Tap ⋮ again, then Bookmarks. Open Mobile bookmarks and find the bookmark you just made.
  4. Tap the ⋮ next to that bookmark, then tap Edit.
  5. Change the name to Copy Web Recipe.
  6. Clear the web address, press and hold in the empty box, and tap Paste.
  7. Tap the back arrow at the top left to save.

Use it

  1. Go to a recipe on any website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the address bar at the top and type Copy Web Recipe.
  3. In the list that drops down, tap Copy Web Recipe (it has a star next to it).
  4. Tap Copy this recipe, then paste it into Recipe Box (see Pasting the recipe below).

Chrome has no bookmarks bar on tablets and phones, and tapping this bookmark from the Bookmarks list will not work. Always start it from the address bar.

Pasting the recipe (tablets and phones)

After you tap Copy this recipe, the button turns green and says Copied! Then:

  1. Open Recipe Box from your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Add recipe.
  3. Under Bring in a recipe from, tap A website.
  4. Press and hold inside the Recipe text box and tap Paste.
  5. Tap Read this recipe.
  6. Look it over, fix anything that came through wrong, and tap Save recipe.

You don't need to change the long jumble of letters at the bottom of what you pasted. That's how Recipe Box knows exactly where everything goes.

If the bookmark can't find the recipe

Most recipe websites include a hidden copy of the recipe that the bookmark reads. A few don't. If that happens, you'll see Recipe Box could not find a recipe on this page.

  1. Tap or click OK to close the message.
  2. Select the recipe's text on the page, from the name down through the last step.
  3. Use the bookmark again.

Recipe Box will read the text you selected instead.

Saving Recipes from a Cookbook

Have a favorite recipe in a cookbook or on a recipe card? On an iPad, iPhone, or Android device, you can scan the words with the camera instead of typing them. Recipe Box then fills out the recipe form for you.

No picture is taken. The camera reads the words right on your device, and only the words go into Recipe Box. It's free and works without internet.

Getting started

  1. Tap Add recipe.
  2. Under Bring in a recipe from, tap A cookbook.
  3. You'll see boxes for the Recipe name, Ingredients, and Steps. Fill each box with that part of the recipe, using the steps below for your device.
  4. If you like, type which cookbook it came from, like Grandma's church cookbook, page 112.
  5. Tap Fill out the recipe. Look everything over in the form, fix anything that came through wrong, and tap Save.

Scanning on an iPad or iPhone

  1. Tap in the box you want to fill, then tap it again so a small menu pops up.
  2. Tap Scan Text. If you don't see it, tap the arrow at the end of the menu. On some devices it's an icon of a page inside a frame.
  3. Hold the camera over that part of the recipe. When the words are outlined, tap Insert.

Scanning on an Android tablet or phone

  • If your keyboard has a scan or camera button in the row above the keys, tap in the box, tap that button, and point the camera at the recipe. On Samsung keyboards it's called Extract text.
  • If it doesn't, open Google Lens, point it at the page, and tap Text. Select the words, tap Copy, then come back to Recipe Box and paste them into the box.

On a computer

Computers can't scan, but the same screen still helps. Type or paste each part into its box, and Recipe Box sorts it into the recipe form.

Tips for a good scan

  • Use good light and hold the page as flat as you can. Pages that curve toward the middle of the book are harder to read.
  • Scan a little at a time. You can scan more than once into the same box. If the ingredients are in two columns, or the steps continue on the next page, just scan each part into the same box.
  • Fix mistakes right in the box before you tap Fill out the recipe, or fix them afterward in the form.
  • Servings and cooking times printed with the recipe can be scanned into the Steps box too. Recipe Box will find them and put them in the right place.

What Can I Make?

Got chicken in the fridge and no idea what to make? Tell Recipe Box what you have, and it shows which of your recipes use it.

Using it

  1. On the Browse screen, tap What can I make? near the top.
  2. In the box, type an ingredient you have, like chicken, and tap Add. As you type, Recipe Box suggests ingredients from your recipes.
  3. Add as many as you like. You can add several at once with commas, like chicken, rice, onion.

The recipes appear below in three groups:

  • Ready to make means you have everything the recipe calls for.
  • Almost there means you're only missing one or two things.
  • Uses some of what you have means you'd need a few more things.

Each recipe shows which of your ingredients it uses, and what you'd still need. Tap a recipe to open it.

Good to know

  • Start with the main ingredients, like meats, vegetables, and grains. You don't need to list everything in your pantry.
  • Simple names work best. "Chicken" matches chicken breasts, chicken thighs, and whole chickens. It won't match things like chicken broth, which is a different ingredient. Add that separately if you have it.
  • Salt, pepper, water, and cooking oil are counted as things you already have. Uncheck I always have salt, pepper, water, and cooking oil if you'd rather count them.
  • Recipe Box remembers your list for next time. To take something off, tap the × next to it, or tap Clear the list to start over.

Printing a Recipe

You can print any recipe on a full sheet of paper, a half sheet, or a recipe card that fits in a recipe box. Recipe Box shows you exactly how it will look before you print.

Opening the Print screen

  1. Open the recipe you want to print.
  2. Tap Print.

Your choices are on the left, and a preview of the printed page is on the right. (On a phone, the preview is below the choices.) The preview changes as soon as you change a choice, so you can try things and see what happens. When you're done, tap Back to recipe at the top.

Step 1: Choose a size

  • Full page, Letter is a regular sheet of printer paper. This is the best choice for most people.
  • Full page, A4 is the standard paper size outside the United States.
  • Half page (5½ × 8½) is half a sheet, a nice size for a recipe binder.
  • Recipe card (4 × 6) is a large index card.
  • Recipe card (3 × 5) is a small index card, the classic recipe box size.

Step 2: Choose your paper (half pages and cards only)

  • Index cards (or Half-size paper) is for when you load actual cards or half sheets into your printer. In the print window, choose the matching paper size.
  • Regular paper, cut out prints on a normal sheet of printer paper, with dashed lines to cut along. Two 4 × 6 cards or three 3 × 5 cards fit on one sheet. Half pages print two to a sheet, turned sideways.

Not sure? Choose Regular paper, cut out. It works with any printer and the paper you already have.

Step 3: Adjust anything else (optional)

  • Servings. Tap − or + to print the recipe for more or fewer people.
  • Include. Uncheck anything you'd like to leave off, such as Tips and notes. Handy for fitting a recipe on a small card.
  • Text size. Fit to fewest pages is best for most recipes. If the text is too small to read comfortably, tap Larger.
  • Look. Recipe card colors prints the colored headings. Plain, saves ink prints in black and white.

Just above the Print button, Recipe Box tells you how it will fit, such as Fits on 1 Letter page or Fits on 1 card.

Step 4: Print

  1. Tap the red Print button.
  2. Your device's print window opens. Choose your printer.
  3. Check two settings so your recipe prints at the right size:
    • Scale should be 100% (sometimes called Actual size or Default).
    • Headers and footers should be turned off. Otherwise, the date and a web address print across the top and bottom.
  4. Tap Print.

Where to find those settings:

  • In Chrome or Edge on a computer, click More settings in the print window.
  • On an iPad or iPhone, tap Options if you see it. Most iPads don't add headers and footers.
  • On an Android tablet or phone, tap the small arrow under the printer name.

Good to know

  • Recipe Box remembers your choices, so the next recipe you print uses the same size and settings.
  • If a recipe is too long for a card, the message above the Print button will say it needs more than one card. Try unchecking Tips and notes or Equipment, or choose a bigger size.

Meal Plan and Shopping List

Recipe Box can help you plan what to cook during the week and make a shopping list for it, all in one place.

The Meal plan

Tap Meal plan at the top of the screen. (On a phone, it's at the bottom.) You'll see the days of the week, Sunday through Saturday, with today marked today. Each day shows what you've planned: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Other.

To see another week, tap the arrows on either side of the dates at the top. Tap Back to this week to return to the current week.

Planning a meal

From the recipe:

  1. Open the recipe and tap Add to Meal plan.
  2. Under Which day?, tap the day. You can choose any day in the next two weeks.
  3. Under Which meal?, tap Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Other.
  4. Tap Add to plan.

From the Meal plan:

  1. Under the day you want, tap Add a recipe.
  2. Under Which meal?, tap the meal.
  3. Tap the recipe from the list. If you have a lot of recipes, type part of its name in the Find a recipe box.

To take a recipe off your plan, tap the small × next to it. To open a planned recipe, tap its name.

The shopping list gathers all the ingredients you need into one list for the store. Tap the shopping cart at the top of the screen to see it. A small red number on the cart tells you how many items are left to get.

Adding to your shopping list

  • One recipe: open the recipe and tap Add to Shopping list. If you changed the servings first, the list uses your new amounts.
  • A whole week: go to the Meal plan and tap Add this week to the shopping list at the bottom.

  • Changed your mind about a recipe? Tap the × next to its name under For these recipes.

When you're done shopping

To start completely fresh, open the shopping list, scroll to the bottom, and tap Clear the whole list. Recipe Box will ask you to confirm first.

Taking your list with you

  • Copy list copies the list so you can paste it into a text message or email.
  • Print prints the list to take to the store.

Sharing a Recipe

You can send one recipe to a friend or family member who also uses Recipe Box. It goes as a small recipe file that they add to their own Recipe Box. Everything comes along: the ingredients, steps, notes, categories, difficulty, and photo.

Your favorites, meal plan, and shopping list are yours, so those don't go with it.

Sending a recipe

  1. Open the recipe you want to share.
  2. Tap Share. It's next to Print and Edit.
  3. On a tablet or phone, a share menu usually opens. Choose how to send it, like Messages, Mail, or Gmail, and pick the person.
  4. On a computer, Recipe Box saves the recipe file, usually in your Downloads folder. It's named after the recipe, like Grandma's Chili.recipe.json. Start an email, attach that file, and send it.

On some Android tablets and phones, the share menu doesn't open. Instead, Recipe Box saves the file to your Downloads folder and tells you so. Attach it to an email or message the same way you would on a computer.

Adding a recipe someone sent you

  1. Save the recipe file from the email or message. On an iPad or iPhone, tap the file, then tap the Share button and choose Save to Files. On a computer or Android device, download the attachment.
  2. Open Recipe Box and tap Add recipe.
  3. Under Bring in a recipe from, tap A shared file and choose the file. (You can also find Add a shared recipe in the settings menu.)
  4. Recipe Box shows you a preview card. If it looks right, tap Add to my box.

If you already have a recipe with that name

Recipe Box will ask what you'd like to do:

  • Keep both adds the new one with (shared) after its name, so you can tell them apart.
  • Replace mine swaps your recipe for the one you were sent. Recipe Box asks you to confirm first. If it was one of your favorites, it stays a favorite.
  • Skip it leaves your recipe alone and doesn't add anything.

Good to know

  • Once it's added, the recipe is yours. Change anything you like, including the difficulty. Your changes don't affect the person who sent it.
  • A recipe file is different from a backup file. A recipe file adds one recipe. A backup file holds a whole recipe box and replaces what's on your device. If you pick the wrong kind by mistake, Recipe Box will tell you rather than erase anything.

Syncing with Google Drive

What syncing does

Normally, Recipe Box keeps your recipes on the one device you're using. If you use Recipe Box on more than one device, like a computer and a tablet, syncing keeps them all matched up. Add a recipe on your computer, and it shows up on your tablet a few moments later.

It works by saving your recipes to your own Google Drive, in a folder called Recipe Box. Recipe Box can only see that one folder. It can't see or change any of your other files.

Do you need it? Only if you use Recipe Box on more than one device. If you only use it on one device, you can skip this section. Just remember to make a backup now and then.

Your recipes stay private. Each person's Recipe Box syncs only with their own Google account, so your recipes are never mixed with anyone else's.

What you'll need

  • A Google account (the same one you use for Gmail, if you have Gmail).
  • Recipe Box installed on each device you want to sync (see Getting Started).

Turning on sync

Do these steps on each device you want to keep in step, signing in with the same Google account every time.

  1. Open Recipe Box.
  2. Tap the settings button (three small sliders at the top).
  3. Find Sync and tap Connect Google Drive.
  4. Google's sign-in page opens. Choose your Google account, or sign in if you're asked to.
  5. Google asks whether Recipe Box can use its own files in your Google Drive. Tap Continue or Allow.
  6. You'll be brought back to Recipe Box automatically.

Settings will now say Syncing with Google Drive, and a small cloud button appears at the top of the screen. You only sign in once on each device. From then on, syncing happens on its own.

The cloud button

Tap the cloud button anytime to see when your recipes last synced.

  • A check mark inside the cloud means everything is synced.
  • Two curved arrows mean it's syncing right now.
  • A line through the cloud means you're offline.
  • A small red dot means Recipe Box needs your help. Tap it to see what's needed.

If you're not connected to the internet, Recipe Box keeps working normally. Anything you add or change will sync once you're back online.

If it asks you to sign in again

This shouldn't happen often, but Google may ask you to sign in again if, for example, you change your Google password. The cloud button gets a red dot.

  1. Tap the cloud button.
  2. Tap Continue syncing.
  3. Choose your Google account on the page that opens.

Nothing is lost while you wait. Your changes will sync as soon as you sign back in.

"Choose which changes to keep"

Sometimes the same recipe gets changed on two devices before they've had a chance to sync. Recipe Box doesn't guess which one is right. It asks you.

  1. Tap the cloud button (it will have a red dot), then tap Choose which changes to keep.
  2. For each recipe, you'll see the two versions side by side: On this device and On the other device.
  3. Under each one, tap your choice:
    • Keep this device's keeps the version on the device you're holding.
    • Keep the other one keeps the version from your other device.
    • Keep both keeps the other device's version and saves this device's version as a separate recipe, with (this device's copy) added to its name. You can compare them and delete the one you don't want.
  4. Tap Use these choices.

Not sure yet? Tap Decide later. Recipe Box keeps both versions safe until you choose.

Syncing right away

Syncing normally happens on its own, but you can tap the cloud button and then Sync now anytime.

Turning off sync

  1. Tap the settings button.
  2. Under Sync, tap Turn off sync.
  3. Tap Turn off sync again to confirm.

Your recipes stay on this device and in your Google Drive folder. They just stop syncing.

Backups and Settings

Opening settings

Tap the settings button at the top of the screen. It looks like three small sliders. Tap Close at the bottom when you're done.

Why backups matter

Your recipes are saved on the device you're using. That's what lets Recipe Box work without the internet. But it also means that if the device is lost, broken, or reset, your recipes go with it. A backup file is a copy of your entire recipe box in one small file. Keep it somewhere safe, and you can always get your recipes back.

How often? Once a month is plenty for most people. Make one any time you've added a lot of new recipes, and always before you restore a backup or empty the recipe box.

Using sync? Your recipes are also kept in your Google Drive. A backup is still a good idea, though. It's a copy you control, no matter what happens to any device or account.

Backup reminders

If you aren't using sync, Recipe Box will remind you when it's time for a backup. A yellow note appears at the top of the Browse screen if you haven't made a backup in your first two weeks of using Recipe Box, or if your last backup was more than a month ago.

  • Tap Back up now to make one right away.
  • Tap Remind me next week to hide the note for a week.

The note goes away on its own once you make a backup.

Making a backup

  1. Open settings.
  2. Tap Download a backup file.
  3. Recipe Box makes a file with a name like recipe-box-backup-2026-10-03.json. The numbers are the date you made it.

Where does it go?

  • On a computer, it goes in your Downloads folder.
  • On an iPad or iPhone, a share menu opens. Tap Save to Files, pick a folder like Downloads or iCloud Drive, and tap Save. You can also choose Mail to email the backup to yourself. If you close the menu without choosing anything, the backup isn't saved.
  • On Android, it goes in your Downloads folder (open the Files app to find it).

Keep a copy somewhere other than the device itself. Email the file to yourself, or save it in your Google Drive.

Restoring from a backup

  1. Make sure the backup file is saved on your device.
  2. Open settings and tap Restore from a backup file.
  3. Find the file and tap it.
  4. Recipe Box tells you how many recipes are in the file and asks Restore this backup? Tap Restore backup.

Please read this before you restore: restoring replaces everything in your recipe box with what's in the file. It doesn't add to your recipes. Any recipe you added after that backup was made will be gone. If you're not sure, download a fresh backup first.

If you use sync, restoring on one device can change your other devices too, once they sync.

Install on this device

If Recipe Box isn't installed yet on the device you're using, settings will show steps for installing it. Installing gives Recipe Box its own icon and protects your recipes, since recipes in a regular browser page can be erased if someone clears the browser's history. If you don't see this section, Recipe Box is already installed.

Sync

This is where you connect Google Drive, sync right away, or turn sync off. See Syncing with Google Drive.

Invite family

  1. Download a backup file to send along with your invitation.
  2. In settings, find Invite family. You'll see a ready-made message with the link to Recipe Box and simple steps for getting started.
  3. Tap Copy message, then paste it into an email or text. (On some devices, you'll also see Share…, which sends it straight from Recipe Box.)
  4. Attach your backup file and send it.

They'll get their own copy of your recipes. From then on, their recipe box is their own. Changes they make won't affect yours, and yours won't affect theirs.

Save recipes from websites

This is where you set up Save to Recipe Box on a computer, or Copy Web Recipe on a tablet or phone. See Saving Recipes from Websites.

Appearance

  • Light uses the bright recipe-card colors. This is the standard look.
  • Dark uses dark backgrounds, which some people find easier on the eyes at night.
  • Match device follows your device's own light or dark setting.

Text size

Makes everything in Recipe Box bigger and easier to read, including the buttons. Choose Regular, Larger, or Largest. The change happens right away, and each device remembers its own setting. Printed recipes aren't affected.

Recently deleted

Shows recipes you've deleted in the last 30 days, so you can put them back. See Adding and Editing Recipes.

The quick tour

Tap take the quick tour near the top of settings to see the short tour of Recipe Box again.

Start over

Empty the recipe box removes every recipe from this device, so you can start from scratch. Your list of ingredients and categories stays. Recipe Box asks Empty the recipe box? first. Tap Empty the box only if you're sure.

The recipes move to Recently deleted, so you have 30 days to put any of them back. Still, it's a good idea to make a backup first. If sync is on, emptying the box on one device empties it on your other devices too.

The "Saved" label

On wide computer screens, a small green label at the top says Saved. That means your recipes are safely stored on your device. (On smaller screens, the label is hidden, but saving still happens the same way.) If it ever says Not saved, your device isn't letting Recipe Box store recipes, which can happen in a private browsing window. Make a backup right away, and use Recipe Box from its installed icon.

Common Questions

Finding things

Some of my recipes are missing!

Don't worry, they're probably just hidden. On the Browse screen, clear the search box, tap All, slide both Difficulty circles back to the ends, and make sure Include recipes not rated yet is checked.

All my recipes are gone on my iPad or iPhone.

Make sure you opened Recipe Box from its icon on your Home Screen, not from Safari or Chrome. They each keep their own separate recipe box.

I can't find a recipe under the letter I expected.

Recipes are filed by the very first word of the name, including "The," "A," and "My." You can also use the search box or the Index.

I added a recipe on my computer, but it's not on my tablet.

Each device keeps its own recipes unless sync is turned on. If sync is on, tap the cloud button and then Sync now, and make sure both devices use the same Google account.

Adding recipes

I made changes to a recipe, but they disappeared.

Changes are only kept when you tap Save recipe or Save changes. Tapping Cancel or the back link leaves without saving.

A pasted recipe came out jumbled.

Some websites have extra text mixed in. Look it over before saving and fix anything in the wrong spot. For long paragraph-style steps, tap Split into separate steps.

Copy Web Recipe doesn't do anything when I tap it.

In Chrome, the bookmark won't work from the Bookmarks list. Tap the address bar, type Copy Web Recipe, and tap the starred suggestion. In Safari, the bookmark's address may not have pasted correctly. Open settings, tap Set up Copy Web Recipe again, and redo the paste step.

It says "Recipe Box could not find a recipe on this page."

Select the recipe text yourself, from the name through the last step, then use the bookmark again.

I pasted a recipe and there's a long jumble of letters at the bottom.

That's normal. Leave it in, and tap Read this recipe.

Cooking

The timer didn't beep.

Make sure your volume is turned up, and on an iPhone or iPad, that it isn't set to silent. Keep cooking mode open while a timer is running.

My screen keeps going dark while I cook.

Use Start cooking. In cooking mode, most devices keep the screen on. If yours doesn't, you can change how long your screen stays on in your device's own settings.

Printing

The date and a web address print at the top and bottom of my recipe.

In the print window, turn off Headers and footers. In Chrome or Edge, it's under More settings.

My printed recipe card is the wrong size.

In the print window, set Scale to 100% (sometimes called Actual size). If you're printing on real index cards, also choose the matching paper size.

Sync and backups

The cloud button has a red dot.

Tap it. It will tell you what's needed, usually Continue syncing or Choose which changes to keep.

I deleted a recipe by mistake.

Open settings and tap Recently deleted, then tap Put it back. Deleted recipes wait there for 30 days. If it's been longer than that, a backup file from before you deleted it will have it. Remember that restoring replaces everything in your recipe box, so download a fresh backup first if you've added recipes since.

I got a new phone or tablet. How do I move my recipes?

Install Recipe Box on the new device. Then either turn on sync with the same Google account, or download a backup file on the old device and restore it on the new one.

General

Do I need the internet?

Only the very first time you open Recipe Box, and for syncing or saving recipes from websites. Otherwise, it works without internet.

How do I get the newest version?

It updates itself whenever you open Recipe Box while connected to the internet. If something looks out of date, close Recipe Box completely and open it again.

Does Recipe Box cost anything?

No. Recipe Box is free, and there are no ads.

Can anyone else see my recipes?

No. Your recipes are stored on your own device, and if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. Nobody else can see them, including whoever shared Recipe Box with you.

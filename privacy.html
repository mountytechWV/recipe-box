Recipe Box privacy policy

Recipe Box is a free recipe app shared among family and friends. This page explains what happens with your information.

Your recipes stay with you

Your recipes, meal plans, and shopping lists are saved on your own device. The people who run Recipe Box have no server that stores your recipes and cannot see them.

Optional sync

If you choose to turn on sync, Recipe Box connects to your own Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox account. It saves your recipes as files in a "Recipe Box" folder in that account, and only reads and changes the files it created there. It does not see or use any of your other files, emails, or information.

Your sign-in happens directly with Google, Microsoft, or Dropbox. Recipe Box never sees your password. The access it receives is kept only on your device, and you can turn off sync at any time from the app's settings menu. You can also remove Recipe Box's access from your Google, Microsoft, or Dropbox account settings.

No ads or tracking

Recipe Box has no ads, does not track you, and does not sell or share your information with anyone.

Questions

If you have questions, contact the person who shared Recipe Box with you.
